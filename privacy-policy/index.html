Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Last updated: September 2026.

Information we collect

When you submit our appointment request form, we collect the contact details you provide (name, email, phone) and your stated area of interest. Please do not include detailed medical information in the form; clinical information is collected only through our secure intake process after you become a patient.

How we use it

Contact information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and schedule care. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Patient health information

Clinical records for patients of the practice are protected health information and are handled in accordance with HIPAA and applicable Virginia and Washington State law. This website is not a patient portal and should not be used to transmit medical records.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. Basic, anonymous usage statistics may be collected to help us improve the site.

Questions

Contact us through the contact page with any privacy questions or requests.
