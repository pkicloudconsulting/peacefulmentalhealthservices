Accessibility Statement

Peaceful Mental Health Services is committed to a website that everyone can use.

We want every visitor to be able to learn about our care and reach us easily, regardless of ability or technology. This site is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA, including:

  • Semantic headings and landmarks for screen readers
  • Sufficient color contrast throughout
  • Full keyboard navigability, including a skip-to-content link
  • Text that scales cleanly with browser zoom settings
  • Reduced-motion support for users who prefer it

If you encounter a barrier anywhere on this site, please tell us through the contact page. We take every report seriously and will work to fix issues promptly. If you need assistance scheduling an appointment in an alternative format, we are glad to help by phone.
