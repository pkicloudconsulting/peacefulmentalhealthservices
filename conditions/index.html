Conditions We Treat

Mental health conditions are medical conditions: common, diagnosable, and treatable. We provide expert, judgment-free care for children, adolescents, and adults living with the conditions below, and with the many ways they overlap.

Depression

From persistent sadness and loss of interest in daily activities to changes in sleep, appetite, and energy. Depression is highly treatable, and you do not have to wait until it is unbearable to seek help.

Anxiety and Panic Disorders

Excessive worry, restlessness, panic attacks, and social avoidance that crowd out the life you want. We treat generalized anxiety, panic disorder, and social anxiety with therapy, medication, and practical skills.

Bipolar and Mood Disorders

Significant fluctuations in mood, energy, and activity, from deep lows to elevated or irritable highs. Accurate diagnosis and steady management make a stable, full life absolutely possible.

ADHD

Challenges with attention, focus, impulsivity, and hyperactivity in children, adolescents, and adults. We provide careful evaluation and treatment plans that fit school, work, and home life.

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

Intrusive, unwanted thoughts paired with repetitive behaviors or mental rituals. OCD responds well to the right combination of therapy and medication.

PTSD and Trauma-Related Conditions

Flashbacks, nightmares, severe anxiety, avoidance, and emotional numbness after traumatic experiences. Our trauma-aware providers help you process the past safely, at your pace.

Sleep Disorders and Insomnia

Poor sleep drains mood, concentration, and energy, and it worsens nearly every other condition. We treat insomnia and related sleep problems as a core part of mental wellness.

Postpartum and Perinatal Mood Disorders

Persistent sadness, intense worry, or intrusive thoughts during pregnancy or after birth. New and expecting parents deserve fast, compassionate, judgment-free support.

PMDD and Severe PMS

Debilitating emotional and physical symptoms in the weeks before a period. PMDD is real, physical, and treatable.

Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders

Hallucinations, delusions, and disorganized thinking are frightening for patients and families alike. Consistent, respectful psychiatric care makes stability achievable.

Eating Disorders

Anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, and binge eating disorder involve far more than food. We provide psychiatric support as part of a coordinated recovery team.

Developmental and Learning Differences

Autism spectrum disorder, intellectual disability, and specific learning disorders. We help individuals and families understand strengths, needs, and supports.

Substance Use and Dual Diagnosis

Compulsive substance use despite harmful consequences, often intertwined with depression, anxiety, or trauma. We treat the whole picture, not just one piece.

Grief and Life Transitions

Loss, divorce, career change, menopause, retirement: some seasons of life ask more of us than we can carry alone. Support through transition is real medicine.

Not sure whether what you are experiencing has a name? That is exactly what an evaluation is for.
