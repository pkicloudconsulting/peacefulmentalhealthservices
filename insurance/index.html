Not sure about your coverage?

Insurance benefits vary by plan, even within the same carrier. Call us at +1 (240) 344-1402 or email office@peacefulmentalhealthservices.com with your member ID, and we will verify your benefits before you book.

Self-pay options are available for patients without coverage or who prefer not to use insurance; contact us for current rates.
