Our Services

Comprehensive psychiatric and therapeutic care for adults, offered online through secure telehealth across Virginia and Washington State.

Adult Psychiatric Services

Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation and medication management for adults. Whether you are seeking help for the first time or transferring care, we take the time to understand your story, your history, and your goals before any treatment begins.

Psychotherapy

Therapy at Peaceful Mental Health Services feels like a genuine conversation, not a clinical interview. Using evidence-based approaches tailored to your needs, your therapist helps you process what you are carrying, build practical coping skills, and move toward the life you want.

Medication Management

Expert evaluation and ongoing management of psychiatric medications. We handle prescribing, careful dose adjustments, side-effect monitoring, and regular reviews so your medication keeps working as your life changes. Our goal: the greatest relief with the fewest medications at the lowest effective doses.

Diagnostic Evaluations

Thorough assessments to understand your condition. Every journey with us begins with clear answers: a plain-language explanation of what we found and a treatment plan you helped shape.

Telehealth Services

Our secure, user-friendly telehealth system brings individual therapy, medication management, and diagnostic evaluations to you, anywhere in Virginia and Washington State. You receive the same high-quality care as an office visit without the need to travel.

All you need is a phone, tablet, or computer with a camera and a private space to talk.

GeneSight Testing

Genes influence how each of us processes medication. A simple cheek swab provides insight into which psychiatric medications your body is likely to respond to best, helping us optimize treatment sooner and reduce frustrating trial-and-error.

Oncology Psychotherapy

A cancer diagnosis affects far more than the body. Our specialized psychotherapy supports patients and families through diagnosis, treatment, and survivorship with care that honors both the medical reality and the emotional weight of the journey.
