Our Services

Comprehensive psychiatric and therapeutic care, individualized for every patient and delivered with warmth. All services are available through secure telehealth across Virginia.

Psychiatric Evaluations

Every journey with us begins with understanding. Our comprehensive diagnostic evaluations go far beyond a symptom checklist: we take time to explore your history, health, relationships, and goals so that your diagnosis reflects the whole picture of your life.

You leave your evaluation with clear answers, a plain-language explanation of what we found, and a treatment plan you helped shape.

Medication Management

When medication is part of your plan, it deserves expert, attentive stewardship. We handle prescribing, careful dose adjustments, side-effect monitoring, and regular reviews so your medication keeps working as your life changes.

Our goal is always the same: the greatest relief with the fewest medications at the lowest effective doses.

Individual Psychotherapy

Therapy at Peaceful Mental Health Services feels like a genuine conversation, not a clinical interview. Using evidence-based approaches tailored to your needs, your therapist helps you process what you are carrying, build practical coping skills, and move toward the life you want.

Telehealth Services

Our secure, user-friendly telehealth platform brings evaluations, therapy, and medication management to you, anywhere in Virginia. You receive the same high-quality, unhurried care as an in-person appointment without travel, waiting rooms, or time away from family and work.

All you need is a phone, tablet, or computer with a camera and a private space to talk.

Child and Adolescent Psychiatry

Growing minds deserve specialized care. We provide developmentally informed evaluation and treatment for children and adolescents, always in partnership with parents and caregivers, so the whole family understands the plan and feels supported by it.

Pharmacogenomic Testing

Genes influence how each of us processes medication. A simple cheek swab can reveal which psychiatric medications your body is likely to respond to best, helping us find the right medication sooner and reduce the frustrating trial-and-error that so many patients have experienced elsewhere.

Pre-Surgical Psychological Evaluations

Preparing for bariatric surgery or another major procedure often requires a psychological evaluation. We provide thorough, timely assessments that satisfy surgical program requirements while genuinely preparing you for the changes ahead.

Oncology Support Therapy

A cancer diagnosis affects far more than the body. Our specialized psychotherapy supports patients and families through diagnosis, treatment, and survivorship with care that honors both the medical reality and the emotional weight of the journey.

Holistic Wellness Planning

True to our mind, body, and spirit philosophy, every treatment plan can include practical wellness strategies: sleep restoration, mindfulness practice, movement, and stress management that strengthen and sustain your clinical treatment.
